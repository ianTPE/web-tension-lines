Hovercard with anchor positioning

Move the mouse onto a link below, or Tab to it, and after a moment a preview card appears. The card stays attached to its link; the rightmost link has no room on its right, so its card flips to the left automatically.

How it works

<a href="/ideas/" interestfor="card-ideas">Ideas</a>
<div id="card-ideas" popover="hint" class="hovercard">…</div>

.hovercard {
  position-area: block-end span-inline-end;
  position-try-fallbacks: flip-block, flip-inline;
}

interestfor lets both hover and keyboard focus show the card, and makes the link the card's anchor; popover="hint" doesn't close other menus that are already open. Previously this took JavaScript to measure coordinates, handle delays, and detect overflow.

Without support

  • Treat it as a progressive enhancement: the trigger is an ordinary link, so browsers without support can still follow it; they just don't show a card.
  • Only put supplemental information in the card, something also available after following the link.
  • For cards in every browser, load the popover and interestfor polyfills as the guide describes; this demo doesn't.
